Nutrition basics

Keep the rules simple.

General evidence-based starting points for adults who lift. These are not individualized medical or dietetic advice.

01

Calories decide the direction.

A cut requires a sustained calorie deficit. A bulk requires a sustained calorie surplus. Treat any calculator as a starting estimate, then use your body-weight trend and training performance to adjust.

02

Hit protein first.

For adults doing resistance training, about 1.6 g/kg/day is a strong evidence-based baseline. Going up toward roughly 2.2 g/kg/day gives a practical buffer.

03

Build the rest around consistency.

After calories and protein are set, distribute carbs and fats in a way you can repeat and that supports your training. The best structure is one you can actually follow.

1

Choose the goal. Maintain, cut or bulk.

2

Set a starting intake. Do not treat the estimate as exact.

3

Track the trend. Compare average body weight over time, not one random weigh-in.

4

Adjust slowly. Change intake when the trend does not match the goal.
